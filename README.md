# pauloribeiro16.github.io — Personal Website & Research Portfolio

Personal website and engineering/research portfolio of **Paulo Ribeiro** — Cybersecurity Researcher at the **CCG/ZGDV Institute** and PhD Candidate in Information Systems and Technology at **Universidade do Minho** (Centro ALGORITMI & CeDRI).

🌐 **Live Site:** [https://pauloribeiro16.github.io](https://pauloribeiro16.github.io)  
💼 **LinkedIn:** [https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulo-ribeiro-/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulo-ribeiro-/)

## Multi-Page Structure

- **[`index.html`](index.html) — Overview / Visão Geral:** Executive summary for recruiters and collaborators, 3-layer thesis architecture, and 6 featured flagship projects.
- **[`projects.html`](projects.html) — Projects (45) / Todos os Projetos:** Complete interactive directory of all 41 public repositories and 4 private systems across 5 categories (PhD & Knowledge Graphs, GRC & Security Tools, AI Agents & Benchmarks, Mobile & Linux Systems, and Full-Stack/Foundations Archive), with instant search and category filters.
- **[`research.html`](research.html) — Research & Thesis / Investigação & Tese:** Deep dive into the AEGIS methodology (extending 4SRS), the Unified Cybersecurity & Regulatory Knowledge Graph (CWE, CAPEC, MITRE ATT&CK, ATLAS, D3FEND + GDPR, CRA, NIS 2, DORA, AI Act), System-1 graph traversal (`Jev`), and empirical LLM benchmarks (CyberMetric).
- **[`about.html`](about.html) — About, CV & Interests / Sobre, Percurso & Gostos:** Professional experience (CCG/ZGDV, CeDRI), education (PhD in Information Systems & Technology, MSc in Physical Engineering — *Physics of Information*), technical skills matrix, working rules, and personal interests.
- **[`contact.html`](contact.html) — Contact / Contacto:** Direct links to LinkedIn, GitHub, institutional email (`paulo.ribeiro@ccg.pt`), and institutional researcher profiles.

## Features

- **Bilingual (PT / EN):** Instant language toggle persisted in `localStorage`.
- **Light & Dark Mode:** Automatic system detection with manual override (`styles.css` + `site.js`).
- **Zero Dependencies:** Pure semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS, and lightweight JavaScript ready for GitHub Pages (`.nojekyll`).
